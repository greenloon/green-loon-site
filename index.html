About the firm

Specialist advisors for a sector in transition

Green Loon Capital advises developers, investors, and industrial producers on the projects driving the energy transition. We work where policy, technology, and capital markets intersect — and where getting the commercial structure right is the difference between a concept and a financed asset.

As a boutique, we engage as a senior, hands-on partner rather than a staffing layer. From first-of-a-kind decarbonization plants to large-scale renewable and infrastructure portfolios, we help our clients de-risk revenue, unlock value from environmental attributes, and access the right capital on the right terms.

What we do

Services

Focused mandates across the commercial and financial lifecycle of energy, infrastructure, and decarbonization projects.

Offtake Structuring

Negotiating and structuring the long-term offtake agreements — PPAs, tolling arrangements, and product sales contracts — that anchor a project's revenue and underpin its bankability for lenders and investors.

Environmental Attribute Monetization

Turning carbon credits, RECs, clean-fuel and production tax credits, and other environmental attributes into durable, financeable revenue — from strategy and valuation through to sale and contracting.

Project Finance Advisory

Capital structuring, financial modeling, and debt and equity raising for infrastructure and decarbonization projects — helping clients reach financial close on the right terms with the right partners.
